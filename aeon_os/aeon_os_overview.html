01 · Product promise

From intention to accepted outcome.

The product is not another controller, repository or chat window. It is a durable agreement about what matters, what may happen and what qualifies as finished.

THE OPERATOR OUTCOME
Describe meaningful work on a card, authorize its boundaries, then return to a durable, independently reviewable result.
  1. Record the preferred route, constraints, repository, outputs and acceptance policy.
  2. Authorize work explicitly—scope, tools, permissions, budget and review are not inferred.
  3. Continue from persisted state in a fresh secretary session, not one giant conversation.
  4. Keep blocked work visible and preserve outcome references after execution ends.
  5. Coordinate five objectives without babysitting five terminals or creating five unrestricted writers.
Bounded attention. Use expensive reasoning at decision points. There is no always-running supervisory reasoning loop.

03 · Durable lifecycle

Progress is a claim with proof.

Step through the objective lifecycle. Each transition records a distinct fact; blocked work interrupts progress without erasing the durable state needed to resume safely.

Stage 01 of 06

NEW

Intent recorded

A meaningful objective exists on an Aeon card. The outcome is named, but no execution authority is implied.

Requires: objective + desired outcome Proves: intent is durable
Explicit dispositions

No hidden terminal states.

An attempt may stop without silently converting the objective into success.

  • BLOCKEDquestion or dependency
  • FAILEDattempt did not deliver
  • CANCELLEDauthority withdrawn
Process exited ≠ objective accepted ≠ independently reviewed ≠ merged or deployed Review is bound to the exact artifact or revision delivered.

Persisted authorization gate: TRIAGED → DISPATCHED requires recorded scope, repository, permissions, outputs, budget and review policy.

Mission Control mapping: Live holds active work and questions; Landing Zone holds review; Done follows operator approval. Mission states are not board-column names, and one attempt cannot close an unfinished workstream.

Safe offline behavior: if the runner is unavailable, approved work remains queued. Opening the website does not start a local worker.

04 · Mission contract

Four choices, independently recorded.

Responsibility, deliverable, route and runtime are separate axes. Combining them into one model name makes routing opaque and authorization impossible to audit.

AXIS 01

Role

Who is accountable for this assignment?

  • developer · quant analyst
  • scout · reviewer · operations
AXIS 02

Objective

What kind of outcome must be delivered?

  • implement · bug fix · recon
  • analysis · plan
AXIS 03

Route

How should authorized work be organized?

  • auto · direct · specialist
  • plan then execute · swarm · execution only
AXIS 04

Engine / model

Where does approved reasoning run?

  • CLI / engine · identity
  • model · environment · capability

Preference is part of the contract.

Respect the operator's preferred route within authorized scope. If policy or capability requires a different route, record the actual route and reason. “Understood” does not automatically mean “send a planner.”

Foundation · truth before scale

Build on what exists. Name what does not.

Hangar already transports work. The missing layer is dependable objective delivery: artifacts, authority, recovery and acceptance—not a new competing launcher.

Existing foundation

Existing
  • Card editor, manual launch and opt-in launch-on-drop
  • Database claim queue and three CLI adapters: Claude, Codex and Copilot
  • Heartbeats, cancellation and process events
  • Mission worktrees, transcripts and result envelopes
  • Session capture pipeline and result handling
  • Kairos questions, board-signal gathering and scheduled synthesis
  • Covenant sources / Note schemas and Swarm domain tools and skills

Behavior still required

Gap
  • Independent review acceptance bound to exact artifact or revision
  • Durable artifact capture before worktree cleanup
  • Code-enforced publish / push policy; prompts alone are insufficient
  • Strict objective-result rules and Mission Control workflow mapping
  • Leases, fencing, bounded retries, recovery and restart reconciliation
  • Normalized telemetry, guaranteed result-to-memory references, budgets and entitlements
  • Authorization-bound Kairos dispatch linkage and bounded secretary turns

Operational health is a gate, not a decorative indicator. Historical runner ports, model configuration, strategy counts and Kairos health are not current evidence. Verify runner availability, supervision and feedback before unattended expansion.

05 · Evidence contract

Every analysis leaves a record.

Every run needs a universal receipt. Research adds a more demanding Note only when the output makes claims that should become reusable domain knowledge.

Code stays in the repository

Artifacts and exact revisions remain the authority for engineering outcomes.

Research stays governed

Covenant retains accepted domain knowledge; not every session is forced into a Note.

Kairos receives references

Only authorized concise summaries and pointers—not uncontrolled copies everywhere.

Governance · bounded autonomy

Initiative proposes. Policy authorizes.

Autonomous scheduling is a pre-approved envelope, not a broad instruction in a role prompt. Permissions must be enforced by code at dispatch and execution boundaries.

01

Schedule an explicit envelope

Approve scope, roles, repositories, tools, data access, budget, limits and expiry before recurring work.

02

Keep recommendations as proposals

recommended_tasks[] remains deduplicated proposed work—not automatic cards, launches or a board flood.

03

Separate workstream from session detail

Use coarse cards, checklist phases and detailed attempt records. One attempt cannot close an unfinished workstream.

04

Gate side effects

Write, merge, push and deploy need explicit authority. A generic research role has no production trading side effects.

Entitlement must be verified

No silent paid fallback.

Use approved included entitlement first. Subscription access is not unlimited compute or automatic approval for company data.

  • Copilot enterprise extra paid usage must be admin-bounded or disabled.
  • Claude first-party subscription CLI access is not a general third-party SDK entitlement.
  • Codex approved workspace credentials are available; policy still controls use.
  • API or Azure routes require an explicit, budgeted alternative.
No volatile price or current account entitlement is asserted in this reference.
06 · Five coherent workstreams

One direction, five delivery fronts.

Each workstream closes a distinct trust gap. Together they produce the five-objective outcome without pretending that simultaneous execution alone is coordination.

  1. WORKSTREAM 01

    Reliable mission delivery

    Retain artifacts, enforce permissions, map workflow states and make acceptance and recovery dependable.

  2. WORKSTREAM 02

    Bounded coordination

    Record role, route and authorization; use a replaceable Astra secretary only at decision points.

  3. WORKSTREAM 03

    Reusable research evidence

    Connect RunReceipt, ResearchPack and Note boundaries with complete provenance and governed acceptance.

  4. WORKSTREAM 04

    Scheduled domain work

    Introduce opt-in nightly research only after policy and recovery gates; retain a separate Swarm / Hive live-feedback track.

  5. WORKSTREAM 05

    Morning decisions

    Offer one compact review inbox for outcomes, blockers, unknown health and decisions—never an invented live mission board.

Independent lane

Swarm live-feedback hardening can proceed in parallel. It supports trustworthy strategy evaluation and promotion; it is not the mandatory first step for generic coding delivery.

07 · Delivery sequence

Earn autonomy in stages.

Decisions before autonomy

Open by design.

These are unresolved policy choices, not implementation details to quietly infer. They gate autonomous mission isolation and scale.

Decision needed

Worktree and checkout policy

Active delegated agents use one branch and checkout, while Hangar currently creates a worktree even at concurrency = 1. Serial capacity does not resolve the isolation policy conflict.

Decision needed

Publication authority

Write, push, merge and deploy are distinct permissions. A possible Hangar exception remains a proposal, not granted authority.

Decision needed

Entitlements and budgets

Confirm organizational identity, approved data use, quotas and paid fallback rules for every provider before unattended work.

Status: direction and design reference only. No scheduler, secretary, service, model-policy change, mission launch, trading action, publication or deployment is authorized or performed here.
