## Phase 4 in plain words

The research is clear on one point: an AI that sets its own goals freely just repeats itself. What works is proposing the next goal from a record of past attempts, and only goals whose outcome can be checked. And agents keep only about half to two thirds of their own promises, so a scheduled job, not the model, checks them.

| Track | What it adds |
|---|---|
| A. Goals of his own | Each night he proposes a goal from his record of past attempts, scored for novelty and whether he can learn from it |
| B. Promise list | Every "I will do X by T" becomes an entry. A scheduled job checks it and raises it with you when it slips |
| C. Approve or veto | Buttons on Telegram. Your answer teaches him what to propose next |
| D. Missions as hands | An approved investigation can start a Hangar mission; its result is the proof. Anything that changes things stays approve-first, and nothing merges on its own |

**Rule carried into Phase 4:** any new thinking must be a Max job with a paid backup, and must be added to a routine's list, or it will always run on the paid key.
