Phase 4 in plain words

The research is clear on one point: an AI that sets its own goals freely just repeats itself. What works is proposing the next goal from a record of past attempts, and only goals whose outcome can be checked. And agents keep only about half to two thirds of their own promises, so a scheduled job, not the model, checks them.

TrackWhat it adds
A. Goals of his ownEach night he proposes a goal from his record of past attempts, scored for novelty and whether he can learn from it

C. Approve or vetoButtons on Telegram. Your answer teaches him what to propose next
D. Missions as handsAn approved investigation can start a Hangar mission; its result is the proof. Anything that changes things stays approve-first, and nothing merges on its own
