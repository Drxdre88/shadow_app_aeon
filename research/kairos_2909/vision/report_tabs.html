The real diagnosis

Kairos is mostly fed by machines. Your own words are meant to be the heaviest signal in the brain, but there are very few of them. So today he mirrors what your agents did, not what you think, want or believe.

That was fine while we were filling the store. It is not fine now that you want him to come alive.

Where we are heading

A chief of staff who:

  1. Remembers everything you and your agents do, with the details that matter (what was built, did tests pass, was it shipped, what did it cost).
  2. Thinks every night, keeps only the ideas that hold up over several days, and drops the rest.
  3. Knows what you believe and why, notices when the evidence disagrees, and asks you.
  4. Sends one good message a day, not a stream of pings.
  5. Later, suggests work he can check himself, and with your yes or no, sends agents to do it.

The deal

  • He does: the capturing, sorting, overnight thinking, remembering and checking.
  • You do: keep your sprint board going with a line of notes per card, and on thin board days give about 5 minutes of voice. Plus a tap or a short answer when he asks.
  • Rule that never bends: your words outrank his guesses. He can never turn his own guess into a belief without evidence from somewhere else, and you can undo anything he learns.

When he really needs you

He comes to you only for things he cannot work out alone:

  1. Two of your stated beliefs clash, or new evidence contradicts one of them.
  2. An idea has survived several nights of testing and now needs a human yes or no.
  3. A goal cannot be checked by a machine, for example "did the traders actually use this?".
  4. A new direction shows up with no reason attached. Your agents have started a lot of work in an area you never said you cared about.
  5. He is about to learn something only from machine evidence. You get a window to veto it.
  6. Cards you finished are a title only, and he cannot tell what they meant. He asks about at most three a day, inside the daily message.

He will not come to you for things he can find in the code, the board or his own memory.

The weight rules

  1. Your deliberate words (reflections, dialogue notes) are beliefs straight away. They carry the most weight.
  2. Your chat is turned into notes and goes into the "maybe" pile, not straight into beliefs. Offhand chat should not rewrite his worldview.
  3. Tool evidence (tests passed, PR merged, card done) is trusted facts about what happened.
  4. His own guesses never become beliefs without outside support.
  5. Confidence comes from the source, not the tone. A confident sentence from the model counts for nothing on its own.
  6. Nothing is deleted. Old beliefs are marked replaced, and you can undo any change.

What makes a good voice note

  • Say the area: "for Hive", "for Swarm", "for Aeon".
  • Say the belief and why: "I think the order pad matters more than the backtests right now, because the desk needs it daily".
  • Say what would change your mind: this is what lets him check you later instead of just agreeing.
  • Corrections are gold: "stop flagging X", "that's parked", "wrong, the reason is Y".
  • Say how you feel about it: "this is dragging", "I'm excited about this". That is signal too.

Three minutes of this a day outweighs a month of machine session notes. A well-kept board day is worth the same, and you are doing that work anyway.

The memory engine

The point: good thinking does not come from dumping notes into a database. It comes from a loop that runs every day: weigh, age, back up, challenge, merge, learn from your reaction. This tab is that loop.

Two numbers on every memory

NumberMeaningWhen it changesWho reads it
StandingHow much he trusts and values this memory overallEvery night, and straight away when you reactChat, daily message, search, galaxy, night thinking
RelevanceHow well it fits the question being asked right nowAt the moment of askingChat and search only

What he pulls up = relevance × standing. Today there is no standing at all: fresh and loud beats true and important. We also have two ranking systems that disagree, and the final step ignores how old things are. The engine replaces both with one.

What goes into standing

IngredientIn plain wordsExample
Who said itYou beat your tools, your tools beat his guessesYour reflection 1.0 · test result 0.8 · your chat 0.6 · his own idea 0.3
How freshEverything fades, at different speedsSession notes fade in weeks, ideas in a month, beliefs in months, your reflections in a year
Used againBeing used or confirmed refreshes itCited in an answer you liked, so the fading resets
Backed upSeparate evidence, on different daysThree sessions on three days agree: strong. He repeats himself three times: counts once
OutcomeWhat happened when it was acted onCard shipped, idea you acted on: up. Vetoed or ignored: down
ChallengedOpen contradictions pull it down until settled"Hive is the priority" vs a week spent on Swarm
NewDoes it add anything he did not already knowNear-copies of existing memories add nothing

Each ingredient is a small separate part. You can tune, test or swap one without touching the others.

StepWhat it doesWhenThinking needed?

Every change is written down with its reason. That is what makes "undo" and "why do you believe this?" possible.

How it is built

  • A class-based engine made of small parts. One conductor runs the loop. Each step is its own class with one job, and plugs in. The standing score is built the same way from the ingredient parts.
  • The maths runs inside the app. Weigh, age, back up and learn-from-you are plain arithmetic: fast, free, fully testable, and they run even if every model is down.
  • The thinking steps plug in. Challenge, merge, contest and check-himself go through one "reasoner" socket. The app prepares the work, a model thinks, and the app checks and saves the answer. The app, not the model, creates every record ID, which removes the whole class of bug that broke the night thinking.
  • The same socket takes two brains. One is the paid API, as today. The other is Claude on your Max plan (next tab). The engine does not care which one answered.
const engine = new MemoryEngine({
  steps: [new Gate(), new Weigh(standing), new Age(), new BackUp(), new Challenge(reasoner), new Merge(reasoner), new Contest(reasoner)],
  standing: new Standing([new SourceTrust(), new Freshness(), new Support(), new Outcome(), new Challenged(), new Novelty()]),
  reasoner, // ApiReasoner today, ClaudeMaxReasoner on your subscription
  changes: new ChangeLog(), // every change + reason, powers undo
})
await engine.runNight(userId)

TypeScript or Python?

TypeScript, all the way. There is no Python in Aeon today, and this engine does not need any.

The one rule that shapes the design

Anthropic allows your Max plan to be used through Claude's own apps (chat, Claude Code, routines). It does not allow Aeon's server to call Claude using your plan's login in place of a paid key.

So Claude comes to the brain for work. The brain never calls Claude on your plan.

Safety rails (all phases)

  • He never gets a goal to keep himself running.
  • A spending and step limit on every job, and an off switch.
  • He asks before touching cards in other people's projects.
  • New powers are unlocked one kind of action at a time, and only after a clean track record.

What you can start today (before any code lands)

  1. Give each AS Sprint card a line of notes as you create or finish it. Dictate it with the phone keyboard mic. It is the cheapest high-value habit.
  2. Voice reflections through the Claude app on thin board days. They already go in at the highest weight. Nothing needs building.
  3. Seed each area with 3 to 5 beliefs. What you are sure of, what you are betting on, and what is parked.
  4. Refresh your 7 objectives. They were last touched on 8 September, and the nightly thinking reads them every night.
  5. Answer his question when one arrives. The block that stops them goes in phase 0.

Why this order

  • Phase 0 comes first because learning from bad input only makes him confidently wrong. He needs to see properly and stop breaking.
  • Memory before beliefs, because beliefs are built from memories that have earned their place.
  • Creativity before drive, because he should only act on ideas that have already survived testing.
  • Your voice runs through all of it. Each phase only helps as much as what you tell him.
