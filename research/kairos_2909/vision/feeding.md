## Your board is the main feed

You already run your day on the AS Sprint board. That is the richest signal you produce, so Kairos should read it properly instead of needing a separate voice note.

| Today | After phase 0 |
|---|---|
| A card you finish becomes "completed · title", with no notes, no checklist and no reason | Finished and vaulted cards are read **in full**: title, notes, checklist, labels, days taken |
| 688 "moved / completed" memories from AS Sprint in 30 days, most of them noise | **One page per board per day**: "what you planned, did and finished on AS Sprint" |
| 214 of the 320 vaulted cards are a title only, so what they meant exists only in your head | **Title-only cards get a nudge**: "3 cards closed today with no notes: X, Y, Z. One line each?" |
| Your answer lives nowhere useful | Your answer is **written back onto the card** and into his memory. The board gets richer too |

**The rule:** on a good board day (cards created, explained and finished) you do not need a voice note. The board speaks for you. On a thin board day he asks at most once, for at most three cards.

**Adding notes by voice, today, no build needed:**

- **Phone keyboard dictation** in the Aeon card editor: tap the mic on your keyboard and speak the notes.
- **Claude app voice** with the Aeon connector: "on AS Sprint, add to the card 'X': ..." (test once, as voice and custom tools had a reported issue).
- **Reply to the nudge** on Telegram, and he files it onto the card.

**How cards are weighed:** a finished card with your notes is strong evidence of what you did and why. A title-only card is weak until explained, because he cannot tell what it meant. Cards you create but have not started are what you **intend**, and he uses them to spot drift between plan and reality.

Step 8 is the one that is missing today. Nothing you do comes back round, so nothing he generates ever gets better.

## The weight rules

1. **Your deliberate words** (reflections, dialogue notes) are beliefs straight away. They carry the most weight.
2. **Your chat** is turned into notes and goes into the "maybe" pile, not straight into beliefs. Offhand chat should not rewrite his worldview.
3. **Tool evidence** (tests passed, PR merged, card done) is trusted facts about what happened.
4. **His own guesses** never become beliefs without outside support.
5. **Confidence comes from the source, not the tone.** A confident sentence from the model counts for nothing on its own.
6. **Nothing is deleted.** Old beliefs are marked replaced, and you can undo any change.

## What makes a good voice note

- **Say the area**: "for Hive", "for Swarm", "for Aeon".
- **Say the belief and why**: "I think the order pad matters more than the backtests right now, because the desk needs it daily".
- **Say what would change your mind**: this is what lets him check you later instead of just agreeing.
- **Corrections are gold**: "stop flagging X", "that's parked", "wrong, the reason is Y".
- **Say how you feel about it**: "this is dragging", "I'm excited about this". That is signal too.

Three minutes of this a day outweighs a month of machine session notes. A well-kept board day is worth the same, and you are doing that work anyway.
