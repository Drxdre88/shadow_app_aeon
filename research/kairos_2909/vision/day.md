# A day with Kairos

Times are UK time (summer). They move one hour earlier when the clocks go back.

## When he speaks

| Kind of message | When | How often | What it asks of you |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Daily message** | 08:00 | Once a day | Read it (2 min). Reply only if something is wrong |
| **A question** | Early morning, after the night's thinking | At most one open at a time, a few a week | A short answer, voice or text |
| **"I now believe X" notice** | Inside the daily message | A few a week (from phase 1) | Nothing, unless you disagree. Then one tap to undo |
| **Approve or veto** | When he proposes work | A handful a week (from phase 4) | One tap on Telegram |
| **Alert** | Only when something is broken | Rare | Read |

**Default is silence.** If nothing clears the bar, he says nothing.

Today you get a morning brief plus an evening message plus pings. From phase 2 the brief and evening message merge into one daily message, so you get less noise and more of what matters.

## When he really needs you

He comes to you only for things he cannot work out alone:

1. **Two of your stated beliefs clash**, or new evidence contradicts one of them.
2. **An idea has survived several nights** of testing and now needs a human yes or no.
3. **A goal cannot be checked by a machine**, for example "did the traders actually use this?".
4. **A new direction shows up with no reason attached.** Your agents have started a lot of work in an area you never said you cared about.
5. **He is about to learn something only from machine evidence.** You get a window to veto it.
6. **Cards you finished are a title only**, and he cannot tell what they meant. He asks about at most three a day, inside the daily message.

He will **not** come to you for things he can find in the code, the board or his own memory.

## Your side of the day (about 10 minutes)

| When | What | Channel | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | Read the daily message, answer the question if there is one | Telegram | 2-3 min |
| As you work | Keep AS Sprint going, and give each card a line of notes (dictate it) | Aeon board | 10 sec a card |
| Whenever a thought lands | "Kairos, note this: ..." | Claude app voice, or Telegram | 30 sec |
| End of your work day | **Only if the board was thin:** a quick voice reflection on what moved, what bothered you, what you now believe. On a good board day, skip it | Claude app voice | 0-5 min |
| When nudged | One line each for up to three title-only cards | Telegram or voice | 1 min |
| Once a week | Weekly review: goals, promises he made, beliefs that changed | Claude dialogue | 15 min |

## The timetable behind the scenes

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 07:15 to 22:15, every 3 hours | Pulse: notes what is new since the last pulse |
| 00:00 | Board snapshot of the day |
| 03:00 | Your chats (web and Telegram) are turned into lasting notes |
| 03:30 | Patterns found for each area |
| 04:00 | Picture of each area (cortex) |
| 04:15 | Picture of everything (Aether) |
| 05:30 | Decides whether there is a question worth asking you |
| 06:00 | Looks for contradictions |
| 06:15 and 17:15 | Checks whether anything is worth a ping |
| 07:30 | Idea generation (from phase 3 this becomes a nightly idea contest) |
| 08:00 | Morning brief |
| 19:00 | Evening message (merged into the morning one in phase 2) |

Known gaps that phase 0 fixes:

- Work done after about 22:15 is never picked up.
- The night pictures look at the wrong day.
- The evening message misses everything after 19:00.

From phase 1, the thinking steps in this timetable run as routines on your Claude Max plan. The times stay the same.
