Kairos: Close the Loops

Kairos has a working brain and no metabolism. Every organ is wired, but not one loop that would let him change his own mind ever closes. The way forward is not to cut organs. It is to close five loops, merge the old-Kairos and new-Kairos duplicates, delete the stubs, fix four live bugs, and give him eyes on the work that actually lands.

Verdict

Keep the whole cognition pyramid. Fix the four bugs, wire the input side properly, then build the five modules as governed loops, not as personalities.

  • What this means: the archetypes → cortex → Aether stack, the intraday pulse, the intent layer and the proposal engine all stay. The Live Mind work stays untouched.
  • The waste is duplication (two ranking stacks, two ask producers, two repair paths, three brief displays) and unclosed loops, not the organs themselves.
  • The weakest part is the input: session capture is the highest-volume writer and the least structured. Missions never write to Kairos at all.

Decisions you own before the swarm launches: ① go on Phase 0 now · ② memory promotions veto-only (Kairos promotes under rules, you can revert) · ③ will you tap approve/veto on Telegram for his initiatives · ④ spec home (docs/kairos/32 or memory only).
